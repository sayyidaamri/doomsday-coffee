Visit Our Cafes
Pre-2020

A coffee lounge with a live music stage.

Mark and Lena run a much-loved music venue and coffee lounge in Palm Hollow. Mark works the room, making sure anyone who walks in feels like a regular by their second visit. Lena is quietly hunting for a coffee good enough to pour for their guests.

The venue years
2017

Lena teaches herself to roast.

No organic coffee on the market clears her bar. So she buys a manual drum roaster and stands beside it. Twenty thousand hours later, her profiles are dialed in to the second.

20,000 hours
Late 2019

Carla & Jules come home.

Mark's sister Carla and her husband Jules move back to Florida from Denver. She's an artist building her own body of work. He's spent ten years building immersive installations for museums. They're figuring out what comes next.

Family reunion
March 2020

The pandemic answers the question.

Everything locks down. Plans collapse. The four of them sit around a kitchen table and bet on the coffee. If the world is going to change anyway, they'll build something worth having on the other side.

The world stops
July 2020

The first cafe opens its doors.

Jules designs the space, the murals, the world of Doomsday. Carla builds the brand and the logos. Mark & Lena develop every drink on the menu. The first cafe opens in the middle of a global shutdown. People come back. Then they bring friends.

Opening day
2023

A second cafe, same obsessive standards.

Demand outgrows the original. The family opens a second destination across town in East Palm Hollow. Same beans, same precision, a brand new corner of the city.

East Palm Hollow
2026

Built by family. Roasted by humans.

Six years in. Two destination cafes. Twelve roasts shipping across the country. The roaster is still hand-tuned every morning. The family still bickers. The story keeps going.
